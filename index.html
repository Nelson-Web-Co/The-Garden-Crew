About the Firm

Legal expertise rooted in Nelson.

Established in Nelson, we have been serving the local community and wider Top of the South region for over two decades. Our team combines deep local knowledge with broad legal expertise across property, family, commercial and employment law.

We believe the best legal advice is clear, honest and focused on your outcomes — not billable hours. Every client matters to us, whether you're buying your first home or navigating a complex commercial dispute.
